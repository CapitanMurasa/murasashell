# My projects
## Perfect Audio Works
### [Github Repository](https://github.com/CapitanMurasa/PerfectAudioWorks)
**Status:** <font color="yellow">temporally discontinued</font><br>
**Uses:** C/C++, Qt framework, Portaudio, libsndfile, FFmpeg, SQLite, Python, TagLib<br>
**Description:** My attempt to recreate Winamp and kinda improve it for modern needs. I really like winamps classical design but every other audio player could not offer what it did offer at that time. For example ability to process flac files (Without external plugins) and more important native linux support! Discontinued due to finnishing core funtionality and there's no demand for my program.<br>
